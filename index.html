Map Memories

Save your memories to the places where they happened, and relive them on a map.

Get it on Google Play

What the app does

  • Pin a memory to any location on the map.
  • Add photos and notes to each pinned memory.
  • Browse your pins later to see where you have been and what you remember.

Your data

Your memories belong to you. Map Memories only uses the data needed to run these features. Read the Privacy Policy to see what is collected, how it is used, and how to ask for your data to be deleted.
